   \section{Definition}
      An action plan specifies when, where, and how an intended action will be
      performed \cite{sniehotta-2006-action-plans-and-coping-plans-for-physical-exercise-a-longitudinal-intervention-study-in-cardiac-rehabilitation}.

      Sniehotta et al. distinguish action planning from coping planning in goal-directed behavior. Action plans specify when, where, and how an intended behavior should be performed. Such plans translate a behavioral intention into more concrete conditions for action execution.
      The study evaluates action planning experimentally in the context of physical exercise. The results support planning as an important mechanism connecting intentions with subsequent behavior \cite{sniehotta-2006-action-plans-and-coping-plans-for-physical-exercise-a-longitudinal-intervention-study-in-cardiac-rehabilitation}.

      An action plan is a detailed plan outlining actions needed to reach one or more goals. \seeherefooter{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_plan}
