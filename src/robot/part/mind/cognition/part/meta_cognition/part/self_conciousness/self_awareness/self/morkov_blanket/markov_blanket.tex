\title{Application or markov blankets in determining self boundries}
\date{}
\author{Mohammad Rahmani}

\begin{document}
   \maketitle
   A Markov blanket provides a statistical boundary that distinguishes a self-organizing system from its external world. The internal states can be associated with the self, whereas external states correspond to everything other than the self. Sensory and active states constitute the Markov blanket and mediate interactions between these internal and external states. This partition provides a formal basis for modelling the distinction between self and other in active inference. The framework is subsequently used to discuss selfhood, theory of mind, and inference about other agents \cite{demekas-2020-an-investigation-of-the-free-energy-principle-for-emotion-recognition}.
